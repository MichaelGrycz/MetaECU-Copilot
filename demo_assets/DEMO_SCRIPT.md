**Format:** screen recording (OBS), English narration, captions burned in.
**Star of the show:** the Interactive Tuning Cockpit. Open it your way: the public Modal Live Product URL (what the judges click — deploy with `modal deploy modal_deploy.py`, see `DEPLOYMENT.md`), the native desktop app `dist/MetaECU_Copilot.exe` (no console window), or locally `python web_app.py` → `http://localhost:8000`. (The CLI `python main.py --intent "Give me 450 Nm, maximum power"` runs the same pipeline headless.)

## Recording checklist

1. Regenerate all artifacts first: `python demo_assets/generate_demo_assets.py`
2. Record the desktop app (`dist/MetaECU_Copilot.exe`) or the Modal URL — never a raw terminal. Window 1920×1080, audio ON (spool on drop, alarm on intercept, relay click on PASS).
3. Keep the rejection moment uncut — it is the product.
4. Show `output/audit_report.md` and the `0xD01FE500` checksum line in the final shot.
5. End card shows the public Modal Live Product URL and the GitHub repo.
